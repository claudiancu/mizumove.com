What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Cookies let a site remember things about your visit, like whether you've seen a particular message before, or help a site understand how it's being used. Some cookies are deleted when you close your browser ("session cookies"); others stay on your device for a set period, or until you delete them ("persistent cookies").

We also use one similar browser-storage technology, described below, that behaves like a cookie for our purposes even though it isn't technically one.

How we use cookies

We keep this deliberately narrow. Mizu Move uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Site, so we can tell what's working and what isn't;
  • let the "Book a 20-min consultation" button open a working scheduling widget; and
  • remember which marketing link brought you to the Site, so if you submit the relocation-plan form, your enquiry is correctly credited to the right campaign.

We do not currently use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or cross-site tracking. If that changes — for example, if we add Google Ads conversion tracking — we'll update this policy first.
