Questions

Before you get in touch

Is this actually free? +
Getting your relocation plan and mover matches is free. You only pay if and when you book a move with a matched mover.
How much does international relocation cost? +
It depends on your route, how much you're moving, and timing — there's no single number that's true for everyone. You'll get a real, itemized quote from your matched mover before you commit to anything, and your consultant will tell you plainly if something looks off. Getting the plan and quote is always free.
How long does international relocation take? +
It depends on the route, customs requirements at your destination, and how flexible your dates are. As a rule of thumb, international moves are easier — and often cheaper — to plan starting 6–8 weeks out. Tell us your date on the form and we'll flag early if it's tight, rather than promise something we can't deliver.
Which countries do you cover? +
We're building out our vetted mover network route by route across Europe. Tell us your specific route on the form above and we'll confirm coverage directly rather than guess here.
How do you vet movers? +
Every partner mover is checked for licensing, insurance, and track record before they're ever matched with a customer. If we can't verify a mover, we don't list them.
Do you help with visas and immigration paperwork? +
Not directly — Mizu Move focuses on the move itself and the practical side of settling in, like customs and timing. Where a visa or residency permit is involved, we'll help you see how it fits into your moving timeline and, where it matters, put you in touch with someone who specializes in immigration law.
I just want moving quotes, not a full consultant. +
That's fine — say so in the notes field. We can connect you straight to matched movers if that's all you're after.
I'm relocating a team, not myself. +
Mention it on the form — corporate and team relocations get routed to a consultant who handles multi-person moves.
